## 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer & Researcher** based in İstanbul, turning large language models and computer-vision research into production systems.

- 🤖 **AI Engineer @ [DT Cloud](https://github.com/tahsinsoyak)** — building tool-enabled LLM agents (planner / retriever / writer / evaluator), an end-to-end **LLM Benchmark Platform for Türkiye's Defense Industries Agency (SSB)**, and self-hosted multi-agent (A2A) platforms.
- 🎓 **MSc Computer Engineering** (GPA **3.96/4.00**) — thesis: *Fine-Tuning Large Language Models for Turkish Agriculture*.
- 🔬 Fine-tuned a domain LLM (Google **Gemma**, LoRA/PEFT) under a **TÜBİTAK** project; passed benchmark-driven milestone evaluations.
- 📝 **Published researcher** in computer vision & applied ML · 🏆 **6× Honour Certificate** recipient · former **Microsoft Student Ambassador**.
- 🌱 Currently deep in **multi-agent orchestration, RAG, LLMOps & evaluation harnesses**.
